 # Lulu Aurelian Estate

## About

Lulu Aurelian Estate is a premium furnished‑apartment platform offering seamless booking, payment, and guest communication for three luxury locations: **Skyview**, **Cocoa**, and **Neema**. The site provides a fully integrated manager portal, guest portal, and automated email workflows to deliver a smooth, premium experience for both owners and travelers.

## Features

- **Dynamic Pricing Engine** – Manager portal price updates instantly sync across the entire site, checkout, and payment gateways.  
- **Cross‑Tab Real‑Time Sync** – Price changes propagate instantly to all open browser tabs via `localStorage` events.  
- **Automated Email Notifications** – Booking confirmations, payment receipts, check‑in details (with Google‑Map pin), house‑rules, and dynamic lock‑box codes.  
- **Customizable House Rules** – Displayed consistently in guest and manager portals.  
- **Google Maps Integration** – Unit locations shown on booking emails and morning check‑in reminders.  
- **Morning Check‑In Reminders** – Automatic 6:00 am emails with dynamic key‑box codes.  
- **Dynamic Key‑Box Codes** – Pulled from manager/agent portals, never hard‑coded.  
- **Flexible Booking Dates** – Dates become selectable again after successful payment.  
- **Guest Cancellation Handling** – Automated cancellation emails with refund information.  
- **Agent Invitation Workflow** – Secure invitation tokens with role‑based access.  
- **Responsive UI** – Modern glassmorphism, vibrant gradients, and smooth micro‑animations.  
- **Multi‑Channel Communication** – Emails, SMS (STK Push), and in‑portal messages.  
- **Comprehensive Dashboard** – Managers can view bookings, payments, and analytics.  
- **SEO‑Ready** – Proper meta tags, semantic HTML, and performance optimizations.  

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Vite, React, vanilla CSS with modern design patterns (glassmorphism, gradients, micro‑animations).  
- **Backend:** Node.js Express, MySQL, custom email service.  
- **Deployment:** Docker‑compatible, CI/CD ready.
